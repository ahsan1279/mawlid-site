Celebrating & showing gratitude for having him
Sūrah Yūnus · Āyah 58
"Say: it is by the Grace and Mercy of Allāh Pāk that you have received His Beloved صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ — therefore it is their duty to rejoice. This rejoicing is superior and better than all other deeds."

The World Prepared
for the Sacred Arrival

When six months had passed with the Leader of the Messengers, Sayyidunā Rasūlullāh صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ — resting in the sacred keeping of His honoured mother عَلَيْهَا السَّلَام — His noble father Sayyidunā ʿAbdullāh عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام passed from this world in the illuminated city of Madīnah. He had been staying with his maternal uncles, seeking recovery from an illness — and it was there that he returned to his Lord.

When the nine lunar months drew to their close within the blessed care of His revered mother عَلَيْهَا السَّلَام, the moment drew near for Him to make His advent upon this world — bringing with Him an endless fountain of mercy and joy.

Heavenly Visitors Descend

On the blessed night of His arrival, noble women from the highest stations descended from Paradise to the presence of His honoured mother عَلَيْهَا السَّلَام to welcome Him:

  • Sayyidatunā Ḥawwā عَلَيْهَا السَّلَام — revered mother of all humanity
  • The mother and stepmother of Sayyidunā Ismāʿīl عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام
  • Sayyidatunā Āsiyah عَلَيْهَا السَّلَام — the noble caregiver of Sayyidunā Mūsā عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام
  • Sayyidatunā Maryam عَلَيْهَا السَّلَام — the blessed mother of Sayyidunā ʿĪsā عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام
  • The Ḥūr al-ʿĪn — the blessed maidens of Paradise

They came from Ḥaẓīratul Qudsiyyah حَظِيرَةُ الْقُدْس — the Sacred Enclosure — to honour the advent of the Beloved of Allāh.

The Embodiment of Light
Made His Blessed Advent

When the sacred moment finally arrived — the moment creation had been waiting for since before time itself — the Beloved of Allāh, the Embodiment of Divine Light, Nūre Mujassam Ḥabībullāh, our Master and the Crown of all Prophets, Sayyidunā Rasūlullāh صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ, made His blessed advent upon the earth — radiant as a brilliant and illuminating Light.

He attained the heights by His perfection  ·  He dispelled the darkness by His beauty
All His qualities are exalted beyond measure  ·  Send Ṣalawāt upon Him and His Family
— Shaykh Saʿdī Shīrāzī رَحْمَةُ اللهِ عَلَيْهِ
