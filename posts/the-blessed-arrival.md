# The Blessed Arrival of the Prophet ﷺ

According to the most well-known and widely renowned traditions, when six months had passed while the Leader of the Messengers, the Seal of the Prophets, the Embodiment of Divine Light, and the Beloved of Allah—our Holy Prophet (peace and blessings be upon Him)—was in the sacred womb of His honored mother (peace be upon her), His noble father, Hazrat Zabihullah [Hazrat Abdullah, peace be upon him], passed away in the illuminated city of Madinah. He had been staying with his maternal uncles from the Banu Adi clan of the Najjariya tribe for a month seeking recovery from an illness, and it was during this time that he left this temporal world.

According to the most preferred and authenticated accounts, when the Holy Prophet ﷺ completed approximately nine lunar months within the blessed care of His revered mother (peace be upon her), the moment drew near for Him to arrive in this world, bringing with Him an endless fountain of joy.

On the blessed night of His arrival, heavenly noble women descended from Paradise to the presence of His honored mother (peace be upon her) to welcome Him:

Hazrat Hawwa (Eve, peace be upon her) — the revered mother of all humanity;

The mother and step-mother of Hazrat Zabihullah (peace be upon them) — father of the Prophet ﷺ;

Hazrat Asiya (peace be upon her) — the noble guardian and caregiver of Prophet Musa (Moses, peace be upon him);

Hazrat Maryam (Mary, peace be upon her) — the blessed and chosen mother of Prophet 'Isa (Jesus, peace be upon him);

The Maidens (Hoor) of Paradise — who all arrived in joy and reverence to greet the beloved of Almighty Allah.

SubhanAllah! (Glory be to Allah!)

When the sacred moment finally arrived, the Beloved of Allah, the Embodiment of Divine Light, our Holy Prophet ﷺ, made His blessed advent upon the earth, radiant as a brilliant and illuminating Light.

SubhanAllah!
