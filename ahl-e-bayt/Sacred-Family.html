The Sacred Lineage — Nasab Mubarak

📚 A Scholarly Note

The chain up to Sayyidunā Hazrat 'Adnān عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام is the most widely narrated, rigorously attested, and well-recorded portion of the noble lineage, accepted by the consensus of scholars. What lies beyond — tracing back to Sayyidunā Ibrāhīm عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام — is known through transmission, though some links invite scholarly discussion. It is presented here with full confidence in the tradition, and ongoing research in this blessed field continues.

اِبْنُ الرَّسُول النُّورُ الأَوَّل سَيِّدُنَا حَضْرَتْ قَاسِم عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام Sayyidunā Hazrat Qāsim The firstborn — An-Nūrul Awwal, the First Light. Our Master صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ took the kunya Abul Qāsim after him. He passed in infancy — and in Paradise, he lives on in eternal light.
اِبْنُ الرَّسُول النُّورُ الثَّانِي سَيِّدُنَا حَضْرَتْ طَيِّب عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام Sayyidunā Hazrat Tayyib The second son — An-Nūrus Sānī, the Second Light. His blessed name means "the Good and the Pure." He was taken in infancy, a brief lamp whose light ascended to Allāh.
اِبْنُ الرَّسُول النُّورُ الثَّالِث سَيِّدُنَا حَضْرَتْ طَاهِر عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام Sayyidunā Hazrat Tāhir The third son — An-Nūrus Sālis, the Third Light. His blessed name means "the Immaculate." Born after prophethood, he too returned to Allāh in early infancy.
اِبْنُ الرَّسُول النُّورُ الرَّابِع سَيِّدُنَا حَضْرَتْ إِبْرَاهِيم عَلَيْهِ السَّلَام Sayyidunā Hazrat Ibrāhīm Born of Sayyidatunā Māriyah al-Qibtiyyah. Named after his ancestor Sayyidunā Khalīlullāh Ibrāhīm. He passed before completing two years. Our Master صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ wept at his passing and said: "The eye weeps and the heart grieves, and we say only what pleases our Lord."

الْبَنَات

The Blessed Daughters
بِنْتُ الرَّسُول النُّورُ الأُولَى سَيِّدَتُنَا حَضْرَتْ زَيْنَب عَلَيْهَا السَّلَام Sayyidatunā Hazrat Zaynab The eldest daughter. She bore hardships with patience in the path of her faith. She passed away in the 8th year of Hijrah.
بِنْتُ الرَّسُول النُّورُ الثَّانِيَة سَيِّدَتُنَا حَضْرَتْ رُقَيَّة عَلَيْهَا السَّلَام Sayyidatunā Hazrat Ruqayyah She emigrated twice — to Abyssinia and then to Madīnah. She was the wife of Sayyidunā 'Uthmān رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ and passed away at the time of the Battle of Badr.
بِنْتُ الرَّسُول النُّورُ الثَّالِثَة سَيِّدَتُنَا حَضْرَتْ أُمّ كُلْثُوم عَلَيْهَا السَّلَام Sayyidatunā Hazrat Umm Kulthūm After the passing of Sayyidatunā Ruqayyah, she too was wed to Sayyidunā 'Uthmān, earning him the title Dhul-Nūrayn — the possessor of two lights.
بِنْتُ الرَّسُول النُّورُ الرَّابِعَة سَيِّدَتُنَا حَضْرَتْ فَاطِمَة الزَّهْرَاء عَلَيْهَا السَّلَام Sayyidatunā Hazrat Fātimah al-Zahrā' The Beloved of the Beloved. The youngest daughter and the most beloved. Our Master صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ said: "Fātimah is a part of me — whoever harms her harms me." She is the Sayyidah of the women of the worlds. Wife of Sayyidunā 'Alī and mother of al-Hasan and al-Husayn عَلَيْهِمَا السَّلَام.
Foster & Milk Mothers · Milk Siblings
أُمِّي بَعْدَ أُمِّي
Ummi Ba'da Ummi — My Mother After My Mother

Among the blessed customs of the noble Arabs was the entrusting of newborns to foster mothers of the desert — for fresh air, pure speech, and strong character. Our Master, the Crown of all Creation, Sayyidunā Rasūlullāh صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ, was honoured with such mothers of the heart.

سَيِّدَتُنَا حَضْرَتْ ثُوَيْبَة

عَلَيْهَا السَّلَام
Sayyidatunā Hazrat Thuwaybah al-Aslamiyyah First Foster Mother · Freed Servant of Sayyidunā Abū Lahab

She was the first to nurse our Master, the Light of the Worlds, Sayyidunā Rasūlullāh صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ, even before he was entrusted to Sayyidatunā Halīmah. She also nursed Sayyidunā Hamzah رَضِيَ اللهُ عَنْهُ, making him a milk-brother. It is narrated that Abū Lahab, who announced her freedom out of joy at the birth of our Master صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ, received a lightening of punishment on that day each week — such is the blessing of joy at his birth.

سَيِّدَتُنَا حَضْرَتْ حَلِيمَة السَّعْدِيَّة

عَلَيْهَا السَّلَام
Sayyidatunā Hazrat Halīmah al-Sa'diyyah The Most Honoured Foster Mother · Tribe of Banū Sa'd

The most honoured and most celebrated of the foster mothers. She came from the tribe of Banū Sa'd ibn Bakr, known for the purity of their Arabic tongue. When she took the blessed infant — our Master, the Best of Mankind, Sayyidunā Rasūlullāh صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ — into her care, the barren land turned green, the dried-up she-camel gave milk, and blessings poured upon her household from every direction. Our Master صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ remained with her in the desert for four years, then was returned to his blessed mother Sayyidatunā Āminah. In later years, she visited him in Makkah and he received her with great honour, spreading his blessed cloak for her to sit upon.

الْإِخْوَة وَالأَخَوَات مِنَ الرَّضَاعَة

Milk Brothers & Sisters

سَيِّدُنَا حَضْرَتْ حَمْزَة Sayyidunā Hazrat Hamzah ibn 'Abd al-Muttalib Milk-brother through Sayyidatunā Thuwaybah — also paternal uncle. Asadullāh — the Lion of Allāh, the Master of Martyrs.
سَيِّدُنَا حَضْرَتْ أَبُو سَلَمَة Sayyidunā Hazrat Abū Salamah 'Abdullāh ibn 'Abd al-Asad Milk-brother through Sayyidatunā Thuwaybah. Among the earliest Muslims.
سَيِّدَتُنَا حَضْرَتْ عَبْدُاللهِ بْن حَارِث Sayyidunā Hazrat 'Abdullāh ibn Hārith (Abu Sufrah) Milk-brother through Sayyidatunā Halīmah al-Sa'diyyah.
سَيِّدَتُنَا حَضْرَتْ أُنَيْسَة Sayyidatunā Hazrat Unaysah bint Hārith Milk-sister through Sayyidatunā Halīmah — daughter of the blessed foster mother.
سَيِّدَتُنَا حَضْرَتْ حُذَافَة / شَيْمَاء Sayyidatunā Hazrat Hudāfah (Shaymā') bint Hārith Milk-sister through Sayyidatunā Halīmah. She was once brought as a captive and our Master صَلَّى اللهُ عَلَيْهِ وَسَلَّمَ recognised her, stood to honour her, and spread his cloak for her.
سَيِّدُنَا حَضْرَتْ أَبُو خَيْثَمَة Sayyidunā Hazrat Abū Khaythamah ibn Hārith Milk-brother through Sayyidatunā Halīmah — son of the blessed foster mother.
